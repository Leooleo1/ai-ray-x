# Code of conduct

raiox is built in the open, and anyone can take part: open issues, review,
send changes. Treat people the way you would at a good workplace.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexual or discriminatory
  language, in any space of the project (issues, pull requests, discussions).
- Do not publish anyone's private information.

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, and block people who keep breaking
these rules.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (it reaches
GitHub and the maintainer privately), or contact the maintainer through their
GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
